My Writing Hand vs. Other Hand Writing Challenge

What We Did

In our class, we compared how many times we could write the same sentence using our writing hand and other hand in 30 seconds. Our professor collected everyone's results and made one table for the whole class. We all used the same table to analyze the data and compare our results. One limitation is that students have different writing speeds, and 30 seconds may not be enough to measure their actual writing ability.

In Plain Words

The one-sample t-test compares the class’s writing-hand average with the professor’s count. The independent-samples t-test compares the writing-hand averages of the Yes and No groups. The paired-samples t-test compares students’ writing-hand and other-hand counts to see whether there is a statistically significant difference.

What I Would Do Differently

This study has some limitations. First, the sample size is small, so the results may not represent everyone. Second, students may have different writing speeds and levels of practice. Finally, the 30-second time limit may affect their performance.
